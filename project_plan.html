🧒 3. Student Portal: Screen-by-Screen Breakdown

Screen 1 (Home Page): Welcome banner with daily moral quote, interactive world religions grid with sacred symbols & theme colors, child's active study streak (🔥), and total Virtue Stars (⭐).

Screen 2 (Dedicated Religion Learning Hub): Clicking any religion launches 5 interactive education modes:

1. 🎬 Video Lectures (Animated Explainer Series) 3–5 min character-driven animated lessons covering history, masters, and values. Features play/pause, speed controls, and auto-marks "Completed" upon 90% watch time.
2. 📚 Moral Storybooks (Read & Listen) Illustrated flipbook stories with step-by-step reading and soothing audio narration with peaceful ambient music for bedtime listening, ending with a 1-line moral takeaway.
3. 💡 Core Teachings & Interactive Flipcards Interactive cards displaying principles on the front and relatable real-world kid examples on the back. Includes an interactive Sacred Symbol Explorer.
4. 🧘 Mindful Calm & Sound Zone 2-minute guided breathing bubble (inhale/exhale visual guide) paired with authentic calming sounds (Tibetan bowls, flute, harp) for peace and emotional balance.
5. 📝 Assessment & Examination Portal Practice chapter quizzes with confetti celebrations, scenario-based ethical dilemmas ("What would you do?"), and a graded Final Exam with certificate generation.

👨‍👩‍👧 4. Parent Portal: Screen-by-Screen Breakdown

Screen 1 (Home Page): Profile switcher for multiple children, executive summary cards (Total study hours, overall average test score, active courses), and mirror grid of all religions with individual progress bars.

Screen 2 (Religion-Specific Progress Dashboard): Deep analytics per religion:

🎥 Lecture Attendance Tracker

Visual completion bar showing completed vs. pending lectures (e.g., 7/10 Completed - 70%) along with timestamps and individual video status.

📊 Weekly Learning Hours Graph

7-day bar chart displaying daily study minutes from Monday through Sunday, with a daily streak counter (🔥) to encourage consistency.

🏆 Exam Scorecards & Marks

Detailed scores for chapter quizzes and final exams, percentage grades (A+, A, B), identifying strength areas and topics needing further guidance.

💬 Family Dinner Prompts

Curated conversation starters for parents to engage with their children at home (e.g., "What did the story of Buddha teach you about showing kindness to animals?").

🎮 5. Gamification & Business Highlights

  • Virtue Points & Badges: Children earn stars and badges for each lecture watched, story read, and quiz completed.
  • Official Certification Engine: High scores on final exams unlock official, printable certificates of achievement.
  • Freemium Business Model: Free introductory lesson & 1st moral story for every religion; Premium Family Pass unlocks full courses, audiobooks, certification exams, and detailed parent analytics.
